Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered

What services does AasanCode Solutions offer?

Pretty much everything digital — custom software, websites, Flutter apps, nopCommerce stores, WordPress design, UI/UX and graphic design, social media management, and IT training. One team handles all of it, so you're not juggling five different vendors.

How much does a website or app development project cost?

It really depends on what you need. A simple business website costs a lot less than a full e-commerce platform or a custom app, obviously. Tell us what you have in mind through the contact form or WhatsApp, and we'll send back a clear quote within a business day.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with startups and businesses both in India and abroad, and we keep flexible hours so time zones don't get in the way.

Are you certified nopCommerce developers?

Yes — our team includes certified nopCommerce developers who've actually built and shipped stores on the platform, not just read the documentation. Setup, custom plugins, theming, ERP integration, all of it.

Do you offer IT training and internships?

Yes. We run college industrial training, internships, and final-year project guidance in ASP.NET, C#, JavaScript, React, Flutter, and WordPress — taught by developers who write this code for a living, not just teach it.

How long does a typical project take?

That depends on what's involved. A basic website might take a couple of weeks; custom software or a full e-commerce platform takes longer. Either way, you'll know the timeline before we start, not halfway through.
